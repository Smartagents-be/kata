This kata teaches you to work with a coding agent, one small step at a time. It starts simple and each step leans on the one before it. The kata is self-paced or guided.

This part of the module explains how the kata works, with examples to work through alongside it.

How to use this document

There are three ways to read these pages:

  • On your own. Work through the text and the exercises in order and learn it by yourself.
  • With a tutor who guides you through every step at the board.
  • As reference. Come back to a page once you have met the thing it describes at your own desk.

A few options sit behind the menu in the top right to configure this course.

Set the assistant to be the one you actually use. This will modify the content of this course to include the relevant commands so you can easily follow along.

How exercises work

You'll find codes written like {f1r5t-5t3p5} during the exercises. A string wrapped in {} marks a flag. Your goal is to capture the flags. These are to be hunted down and typed back into a box in your browser.

Hints

Every answer box carries a Hint button. Press it when you are stuck and it will help you out.

In class, ask your tutor before you reach for the Hint. They can see where the whole room is stuck and can steer you faster than a line of text.
