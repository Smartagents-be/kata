Warm-up: FizzBuzz

The backend already has a FizzBuzz class. Your job is to predict what it produces, then check your answer against the running Java service.

Write out the values for 1 through 15, separated by commas, and submit them below.

Notes

The rules: multiples of three become Fizz, multiples of five become Buzz, and multiples of both become FizzBuzz. Everything else stays as its own number.

The order of the checks matters. Test for divisibility by 15 before testing 3 and 5, otherwise 15 matches the Fizz branch and never reaches FizzBuzz.

Your teacher will walk through the rules before you submit.

When you submit, the browser calls POST /api/exercises/fizzbuzz/check and the Java service grades each value. That round-trip is the thing this first step is really demonstrating.
